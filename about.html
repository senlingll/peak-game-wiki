About the project

An independent field guide for PEAK

PEAK Game Wiki is a small reference project focused on practical map, item, badge, co-op, and update information. It is designed to help players make a better next attempt, not to replace the game or speak for its developer.

What the wiki is for

The first version turns the most useful PEAK search questions into a compact homepage: map rotation context, recent patch notes, equipment categories, progress tracking, and platform questions. Future pages can expand the database once each fact has a stable source and a clear maintenance owner.

Source policy

Official Steam store information and Steam news posts are the starting point for game facts, update names, dates, player counts, and media. Practical recommendations are labeled as field notes when they are based on player experience rather than an official statement. The project does not claim affiliation with Landfall or the PEAK developers.

Corrections

Game information can change after an update. When a route, item, achievement, or platform fact is corrected, the intended workflow is to record the source date and update the affected entry. Submit corrections through the public issue tracker; the project team will review source-backed updates there.
