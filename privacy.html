Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy describes the production PEAK Game Wiki and the services used to understand site usage and support advertising.

Information collected

The site does not require an account and does not ask for a name, email address, game identifier, or uploaded file. The item search and filters run in the browser and are not sent to a server. Hosting and security providers may process standard request data such as an IP address, browser type, and timestamp.

Analytics with Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity is used to understand navigation, scrolling, and usability problems. Clarity may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect pseudonymous device and interaction data. Review Microsoft’s privacy statement for its processing details and available controls.

Google advertising

Google AdSense may display advertising on this site. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to measure ads and show personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on consent and regional settings. Users can manage advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings and control cookies through browser settings. Where legally required, consent choices are handled before personalized advertising is used.

Third-party links

The site links to Steam, GitHub, Microsoft, and Google pages. When you open an external link, the external service receives the information described by its own privacy policy. This site does not control those services.

Changes

Material changes to analytics, advertising, or data collection will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. This policy was updated for the production launch on 16 August 2026.
